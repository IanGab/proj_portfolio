About Me

Hi, I'm a third-year Computer Science student who's still learning the ropes of web development, but genuinely enjoys every part of the process. I love building software applications, from figuring out the logic behind how things work to designing how they look and feel. There's something satisfying about turning an idea into something functional, and I'm always looking for ways to improve my skills with each new project I take on. I'm still early in my journey, but I'm excited to keep learning, building, and exploring everything web development and computer science have to offer.
